About

Currently, I am a researcher in Dr. Jiadong Zang’s group, where I focus on correlated magnetism. My work involves investigating the magnetic properties of transition metal oxides and analyzing how geometric structure correlates with magnetic crystalline anisotropy to facilitate the design of novel hard and soft magnetic materials. Previously, I collaborated with Dr. Shawna Hollen to model quasiparticle scattering in 2D materials and worked with Dr. Ningyu Liu to develop a calibration model for the RIFTS lightning interferometer

I have a lot of interests outside of physics which I try to write about here when I have the time. I'm very interested in teaching and communicating topics to students and the general public.
